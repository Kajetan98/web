Project / 01 — Diagnostics

Cerebral haemodynamics measured without hospital equipment.

AURORA is an autonomous, multimodal diagnostic system for non-invasive monitoring of body-fluid shifts towards the head and for assessing cerebral haemodynamics.

How it works

The system combines a miniature ultrasound with mechanical probe positioning, a NIRS module, impedance cardiography (ICG) and smart compression stockings (GCG) into a single ecosystem. Instead of several separate, bulky machines, one set does the work — with data from every modality synchronised to sub-millisecond accuracy and analysed by AI algorithms.

It operates fully autonomously and set-up time is down to three minutes, which is what makes it usable both on board and in emergency situations where there is no time to calibrate equipment.

Applications

  • Space medicine — studying SANS syndrome in astronauts
  • Emergency medicine — early stroke diagnosis
  • Clinical diagnosis of brain injury

Project / 02 — Wearable

An epileptic seizure detected before anyone can react.

EPI is an integrated wearable medical device designed for early detection and monitoring of epileptic seizures.

Sensor fusion instead of a single signal

The system combines kinematic sensors with optical heart-rate and blood oxygen (SpO₂) measurement. Analysing sudden pulse changes, saturation drops and the characteristic body tremors at the same time lets it identify both tonic-clonic and focal seizures while minimising false alarms — the most common problem with devices that rely on one type of sensor.

Build and safety

  • Fully sealed, shock-resistant housing — safe to wear through a seizure
  • Qi inductive charging, compatible with a standard smartwatch charger
  • A physical SOS button to call for help manually when an aura is felt
  • The option to cancel a false alarm before any notification goes out

Mobile app

A dedicated app is an integral part of the system: it monitors the patient in real time, automatically records detailed biomedical parameters from every seizure for the treating physician, and immediately notifies the patient's relatives when a risk is detected.

EPI app

Live view, the cancellation window and seizure history

A prototype of the companion app, built from the system concept: a BLE service carries device state, heart rate, SpO₂ and battery level, and every event is stored locally together with the signal trace from 30 seconds before the seizure to 60 seconds after it.

Beyond the mockup there is a working build: it runs the detection state machine in the browser, analyses the phone's accelerometer signal, stores events together with their signal trace and keeps a list of emergency contacts. It also pairs with a BLE heart-rate sensor and installs on a phone as an app.
