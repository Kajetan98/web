About / 01

We start with the person, not the device.

The further you are from a hospital, the more depends on data gathered beforehand and decisions made on the spot. We combine space engineering with emergency medicine to close that distance.

Technology / 02

One platform, three layers of health protection.

From the sensor on the skin to the console of a physician consulting from Earth — every part of the system is designed together, not bolted on afterwards.

01

Wearable biosensors

A wristband and adhesive sensors track heart rate, oxygen saturation, temperature and hydration without restricting movement inside a suit.

  • Continuous measurement, low power draw
  • Offline operation with data buffering
  • Calibrated for microgravity
02

Clinical analysis engine

The model detects deviations from each crew member's individual baseline and ranks alerts by urgency.

  • Thresholds matched to individual physiology
  • Recommendations aligned with medical protocols
  • Full decision log for post-mission audit
03

Telemedicine console

The medical team on Earth sees the same data as the onboard system, with transmission delay accounted for in the interface.

  • Transmission resilient to link dropouts
  • Patient history and trend view
  • Encrypted medical data
Process / 03

Deployment, step by step.

  1. 01

    Mission and environment analysis

    We map the operating conditions — mission duration, link availability, crew composition and the medical risks specific to the environment.

  2. 02

    Hardware and threshold setup

    We select the sensor set and establish an individual baseline for every crew member.

  3. 03

    Integration with the medical team

    We connect the system to the ground console and train both the medical team and the crew on handling alerts.

  4. 04

    Monitoring and continuous improvement

    Once the mission is underway we analyse telemetry and update the models as new observations come in.
